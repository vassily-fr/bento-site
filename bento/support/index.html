Frequently asked questions

Where are my projects?
On your device, and nowhere else. They survive app updates, but not uninstalling: deleting Bento deletes its projects.

Do my photos go anywhere?
No. Bento has no network connection at all. See the privacy policy.

How do I send photos to Bento from another app?
Through the iOS share sheet: select your photos, tap Share, then Bento.

Can I recover a deleted project?
No. Deleting is permanent, and asks for confirmation first.
