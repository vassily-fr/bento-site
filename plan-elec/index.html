Plan Élec

Your home's electrical plan, in the browser.

Load the architect's plan, place sockets, switches, lights and shutters on it, and link each switch to what it controls. Plan Élec splits the circuits, counts the materials, then prints the electrician's plan and a checklist to tick off on site.

  • Free
  • No account

  • On a computer

The interface is in French, and the circuit rules follow the French NF C 15-100 standard.

On your plan

Drag items onto the plan image and set the scale by tracing a known wall: lengths are in metres.

Switches and two-way

Link switches, lights and shutters, draw the conduits. Selecting a point shows its whole network.

Circuits

Automatic split of sockets, lighting and shutters, overload warnings, number of breakers.

Materials list

Sockets, switches, metres of conduit and wire by cross-section, speaker cable counted separately.

Several floors

One plan per floor, a name for each point (“TV socket”), mounting heights, undo at any time.

Your plans stay with you

No account. The project saves itself in your browser, and in a file on your computer if you want.
